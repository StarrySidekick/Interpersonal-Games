Every piece in the game, weakest first by power on the 6 × 6 board (the queen is 9). The record is the real fairy chess piece and its Betza notation; the name and words are what the game shows, and yours to change. Turn a piece off to keep it out of every deal.

A mode is a collection of settings. Each mode below shows only the settings it uses, at its preset. Change one and it is marked; the game takes it as that mode's new preset.
