Twenty-Twenty

You're both guessing. And each of you is allowed to lie, once.

One of you says this code out loud. The other types it in. Same code, same deck.

And one of you takes the left, the other the right. You'll be dealt different things.

How it works

You each get a secret thing. You take turns, one question each, and you're both trying to guess the other's. A guess costs your turn — get it wrong and they get a free question.

And you each get one lie. Exactly one, any time, and you never have to use it. So when you answer, they aren't only narrowing a category — they're wondering whether that pause was thinking or lying.

If you think they just lied, call it. Right, and you take the turn back. Wrong, and you lose one.

The phone keeps your secret, your lie and your count. Everything else happens out loud.
