Adventure

Down through the Grove, one board at a time. Take every piece of theirs in as few moves as you can. Beat par and you get more to choose from after each board. How deep can you go?

How to play

Tap one of your pieces to pick it up. Gold squares are where it can move, red ones what it can take, and purple ones are where one of theirs could take it next. Tap a square to move there.

Grey statues never move. Purple pieces, deeper down, move after you, one of them each turn, and take yours. A piece of yours that is taken is gone for the rest of the run; lose them all and the run is over.

Rabbits belong to no one. Each hops in its own pattern after your move, turning round at an edge, and nobody can take one. Footprints show where it hops next.

Every board has a par. Clear it over par and you get an upgrade; on par, a choice of two; under par, a choice of three. Upgrades are new pieces (up to four), suits and weapons, as in the Grove's descent.

Once a board you can start it over, if a move went wrong.
