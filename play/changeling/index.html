Your days

How to play, and how today's pieces move

You are the glowing piece. Every move takes a piece, and you become the piece you take. Take them all.

Tap your piece to pick it up: the pieces it can take turn red. Tap one to move there and take it. Your piece stays picked up for the next move.

Tap any other piece to see how it moves. The star marks today's stranger.

Stuck, or regretting a move? Step back as far as you like. The first step back starts your next try; you get six. Your score is the try you clear it on.

Holes in the board stop sliding pieces. Jumping pieces fly over them.

Mondays are gentle and the week gets harder to Sunday. Every day has one fairy piece, a stranger from the long history of chess.

Test mode

Every dealt day. Green: cleared. Grey: not cleared. Gold edge: started. Test mode keeps its own days, apart from your real ones.
