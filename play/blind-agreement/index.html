Blind Agreement

You both have to pick the same thing. Miss, and both your picks come off the board.

One of you says this code out loud. The other types it in. Same code, same game.

How it works

You're on the phone together. Twelve options, the same twelve on both screens. Pick one without saying which — then say it out loud on three. Match and the round is over. Miss and both of your picks are struck off, and you go again with what's left. Your score is how many passes it took.

You're allowed to say anything you like beforehand. How much you give away is between the two of you.
