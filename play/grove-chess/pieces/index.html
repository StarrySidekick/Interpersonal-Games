The pieces

Every piece in the game: how it moves, how strong it is, where it comes from. Tap one to read it, keep it out of the game, or write how it should change.

Invent a piece

Write its moves in Betza notation (W one step straight, F one step diagonally, N a knight’s L, D and A two steps; double a letter to slide, so WW is a rook; m moves only, c captures only, f forward only, n can be blocked, p hops a screen, g hops like a grasshopper). It is a real piece once saved: Chaos can deal it.
