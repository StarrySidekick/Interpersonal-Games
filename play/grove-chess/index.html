How to play

Catch the rabbit by moving one of your pieces onto its square. You move, then the rabbit hops. It follows a pattern that repeats, and its tracks are numbered on the board so you can work the pattern out. If it lands on one of your pieces, it eats it. It bounces off the edges, and if a stump or bramble is in its way it waits.

Tap a piece to light up where it can go. Tap Pieces to read what every piece does. Waiting spends a move. You have 15 moves before dusk.

Par is the fewest moves the solver needed, and the solver already knew the pattern. You have to find it, so par is hard. Moving two pieces together can sometimes beat it.

One board a day, the same for everyone. Your game is saved in this browser and in the links you send, and nowhere else.
