How to play

Catch the rabbit by moving one of your pieces onto its square. You move, then the rabbit hops. It follows a pattern that repeats, and its tracks are numbered on the board so you can work the pattern out.

Some days the ground crumbles: every square you move a piece off falls away behind it, and nothing can stand there again. Some days the board shrinks: every two moves a square on its edge drops into the dark, at random or round in a spiral, and the square that goes next is shadowed.

Each kind of rabbit has its own colour. Catch one and from then on its kind wears that colour, so you will know it on sight.

Tap a piece to light up where it can go. Tap Pieces to read what every piece does. Waiting spends a move. You have 15 moves before dusk.

Par is the fewest moves the solver needed, and the solver already knew the pattern. You have to find it, so par is hard. Moving two pieces together can sometimes beat it.

One board a day, the same for everyone. Your game is saved in this browser and in the links you send, and nowhere else.
