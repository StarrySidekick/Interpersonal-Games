# Choosing a game

Picking well matters more than playing well. Three questions, in order.

## 1. How much time is real?

Not how much time you have — how much time you'll actually still be awake and
interested for.

| You have | Play |
|---|---|
| 5 minutes | [Rose, Bud, Thorn](../games/rose-bud-thorn.md), [Category Ping-Pong](../games/category-ping-pong.md) |
| 15 minutes | [Twenty Questions](../games/twenty-questions.md), [Ghost](../games/ghost.md), [Two Truths and a Hope](../games/two-truths-and-a-hope.md), [Show Me](../games/show-me.md) |
| An hour, unstructured | [The First Time I…](../games/the-first-time-i.md), [Alphabet Anything](../games/alphabet-anything.md), [Would You Rather](../games/would-you-rather-and-why.md) |
| An evening you planned | [The 36 Questions](../games/thirty-six-questions.md) |
| No shared time at all | [Photo Tennis](../games/photo-tennis.md) |

## 2. What's the energy?

Be honest about the *lower* of the two of you.

- **Wiped out, in bed, half-asleep** → [Alphabet Anything](../games/alphabet-anything.md).
  It's cooperative and asks for nothing. This is the correct answer far more
  often than people admit.
- **Flat, but not tired** → [Category Ping-Pong](../games/category-ping-pong.md)
  for two rounds to get the blood moving, then pick something else.
- **Awake and up for it** → [Ghost](../games/ghost.md) if you want teeth,
  [Fortunately, Unfortunately](../games/fortunately-unfortunately.md) if you
  want to laugh.
- **One of you has had a bad day** → [Rose, Bud, Thorn](../games/rose-bud-thorn.md),
  and then probably no game at all. See rule 3 in
  [house-rules.md](house-rules.md).
- **Deliberate, planned, both up for something real** → [The 36 Questions](../games/thirty-six-questions.md).

## 3. What's the medium?

| Medium | Best games | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice only** (phone, walking, driving) | Ghost, Twenty Questions, Category Ping-Pong, The First Time I…, Fortunately/Unfortunately | Show Me |
| **Video** | Show Me, Two Truths and a Hope, The 36 Questions | Ghost — spelling out loud is better without a lagging video feed |
| **Text, same time** | Twenty Questions, Would You Rather, Fortunately/Unfortunately | anything timed |
| **Text, different time zones** | Photo Tennis, Rose/Bud/Thorn as a daily message, async Fortunately/Unfortunately | anything turn-based and fast |

## The default

If you're not sure: [Rose, Bud, Thorn](../games/rose-bud-thorn.md) to open,
then [Alphabet Anything](../games/alphabet-anything.md) until one of you is
too sleepy to continue. It works on almost any night, which is more than can
be said for most of the more interesting games here.

## The one real mistake

Picking a demanding game because you feel like the call *should* be meaningful.
Closeness comes from playing the easy game often, not from playing the deep
one occasionally.
