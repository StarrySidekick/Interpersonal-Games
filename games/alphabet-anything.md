# Alphabet Anything

> Go A to Z through a category, together, taking turns. No winner, no timer.
> The most low-effort game here, and the one you'll play the most.

**Works over:** voice, text
**Time:** 10 min
**Energy:** very low — this is a game for lying down
**Players:** 2

## How to play

1. Pick a category. *Things you'd find in a hardware store. Bands. Ways to
   cook an egg. Reasons to leave a party early.*
2. Player A does **A**, player B does **B**, and so on to **Z**.
3. Stuck? Your partner helps. This is a co-op game — there is no losing.
4. `Q`, `X`, and `Z` are where all the fun is. Let each other cheat there.

## The rules that matter

- **It's cooperative.** The instinct is to make it competitive; don't. The
  pleasure of this game is that you can play it while half-asleep, and that
  only works if nobody is keeping score.
- **Cheating is legal at Q, X, and Z.** "X — *ex*-boyfriend." Fine. Applaud it.
- **Say the letter first.** "F — frying pan." Keeps you both oriented.

## Variations

- **Alphabet Restaurant** — the category is a meal. You're ordering, A to Z,
  and by the letter M you have built a genuinely disgusting dinner.
- **Our alphabet** — the category is your shared history. A place, a person, a
  joke, a fight, a good day, for every letter. This one takes an hour and is
  worth it.
- **Backwards** — Z to A. Surprisingly different.

## Why it works

Most games ask for effort you don't always have. This one asks for almost
none, which makes it the game for the end of a long day, when you want to be
on the phone with someone but you don't want to *perform*. Being able to be
low-energy together is a real form of closeness.
