# Category Ping-Pong

> Name things in a category, back and forth, five seconds each. First one to
> stall loses. It takes ninety seconds and it wakes a call up.

**Works over:** voice
**Time:** 5 min (rounds are ~1 min each)
**Energy:** low to play, high to win
**Players:** 2

## How to play

1. One of you names a category. *Breakfast cereals. Countries in Africa.
   Movies with a number in the title.*
2. Alternate naming things in it. No repeats.
3. You have **five seconds**. Count out loud — "five, four, three…" — it's much
   more fun than a silent timer.
4. Stall, repeat, or name something that doesn't fit, and you lose the round.
5. The loser picks the next category. This is a real advantage and it keeps the
   game even.

## The rules that matter

- **Count out loud.** The pressure is the game. Silence is not pressure.
- **Loser picks next.** Otherwise whoever is better at this just keeps winning
  in their strongest categories.
- **Challenges are allowed and cost you.** If you challenge a doubtful answer
  and you're wrong, you lose the round. Keeps people from challenging
  reflexively.

## Variations

- **Categories about you** — "things in my kitchen," "people we both find
  annoying," "places we've eaten together." Turns a warm-up into a memory game.
- **Alphabetical** — each answer must start with the next letter of the
  alphabet. Much harder, lasts longer.
- **Deliberately impossible** — pick a category one of you knows nothing about
  and watch them flail generously. This is a bit, not a competition.

## Why it works

It's the opener. Three rounds of this and the call has energy in it, which
makes everything after it easier. Use it when one of you got on the phone
tired.
